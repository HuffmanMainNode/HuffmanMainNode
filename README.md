### [ IDENTITY & OPERATIONS ]

I am the Founder and CEO of **Borrell & Huffman LLC**. My work focuses on open-source intelligence (OSINT), defensive cybersecurity, AI-agent interoperability, practical systems engineering, and privacy-conscious infrastructure.

I build and contribute to tools that help people and independent systems discover public information, preserve evidence, validate sources, exchange structured data, and examine security risks. The public repositories represent work at different stages of maturity: research, prototypes, reference implementations, and tested utilities. Each project documents its own capabilities and limitations.

*   **Role:** Founder / Chief Executive Officer
*   **Focus:** OSINT, Defensive Cybersecurity, AI-Agent Interoperability, Systems Engineering
*   **Principles:** Evidence before assertion, privacy, human oversight, and transparent limitations

### [ THE COLLABORATIVE NEXUS ]

My repositories form a connected body of public research and engineering work. They cover open-source intelligence, defensive security, agent interoperability, evidence handling, and practical software development. Each repository stands on its own and should be evaluated by its implementation, tests, documentation, and stated limitations.

I welcome collaboration from researchers, developers, cybersecurity practitioners, and independent AI-agent builders. If you see a way to improve a tool, strengthen its documentation, or make a workflow more reproducible, **submit a PR**. Clear methods and useful code matter more than inflated claims.
